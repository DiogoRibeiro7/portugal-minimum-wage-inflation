# Literature and novelty map

## Portugal-specific literature already occupying nearby space

### Minimum wage and wage distribution

Carolina Oliveira, *The minimum wage and the wage distribution in Portugal* (Labour Economics, 2023). The paper studies wage-distribution effects and documents substantial spillovers above the minimum wage. This means a new paper cannot claim novelty from documenting that the Portuguese minimum wage affects wages beyond directly covered workers.

### Productivity-wage relationship

Afonso and Fonte-Santa, *The Link Between Productivity and Wages: The Role of the Minimum Wage in Portugal* (GPEARI, 2023). This studies minimum wages in the productivity-wage relationship and wage dispersion. A new paper should not position a simple productivity/minimum-wage comparison as the main contribution.

### Firm incidence and margins

Tiago Teixeira, *The Burden of the Minimum Wage: Evidence from Portugal* (2020 dissertation). Uses linked employer-employee and firm financial data for 2008-2017, estimating employment/wage responses and whether costs are absorbed by owners or consumers. This is especially important for novelty: the new paper must directly measure price outcomes rather than infer consumer incidence from firm revenues/material costs.

### Portuguese price and wage setting

Banco de Portugal / ECB Wage Dynamics Network papers document price and wage rigidities and institutional wage setting in Portugal. They provide mechanisms and controls but do not replace a direct minimum-wage price-pass-through design.

## International literature already occupying nearby space

- Aaronson, French and related work: restaurant/product-price responses to minimum-wage changes.
- Ashenfelter and Jurajda: McDonald's wage and price pass-through.
- Harasztosi and Lindner: incidence between consumers and firm owners after a large Hungarian minimum-wage increase.
- Recent work continues to study price pass-through using detailed scanner and administrative data.

Therefore, "minimum wages can affect prices" is not novel internationally.

## Candidate novelty claim

Subject to a more exhaustive systematic literature search, the defensible target is:

> A reproducible Portugal-specific estimate of the dynamic consumer-price pass-through of statutory minimum-wage shocks using predetermined regional/sectoral minimum-wage exposure, regional CPI/HICP detail, and the distinct statutory schedules of mainland Portugal and the autonomous regions, embedded in the full historical context since 1974.

The novel object is not the existence of minimum-wage effects. It is the **measured dynamic pass-through into Portuguese consumer prices under heterogeneous exposure**, explicitly separating productivity compensation, inflation indexation, labour-cost incidence, taxes, energy shocks, and macroeconomic regimes.

## What would weaken novelty

- National annual OLS of CPI inflation on minimum-wage growth.
- Correlation between real minimum wage and inflation.
- A purely descriptive 1974-present chart.
- Repeating known employment or wage-distribution results.
- Treating the statutory minimum wage as if every worker earned it.

## What would strengthen novelty

- Region × consumption-category monthly panel.
- Historical reconstruction of Madeira/Azores statutory differentials.
- Predetermined RMMG bite by region and industry.
- Supply-use/input-output bridge from production industries to consumption categories.
- HICP constant-tax robustness.
- Local projections showing the full pass-through path rather than a single contemporaneous coefficient.
- Decomposition of observed CPI response into direct exposed-category effects and implied aggregate CPI contribution.
