# Data dictionary

| Variable | Frequency | Geography | Definition | Preferred source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `minimum_wage` | annual/event | mainland/regions | Statutory guaranteed minimum monthly remuneration | DGERT / legislation / regional gazettes |
| `minimum_wage_growth` | annual/event | mainland/regions | Exact percentage change in statutory wage | derived |
| `cpi_index` | monthly | national/NUTS II | Consumer Price Index | INE |
| `hicp_index` | monthly | national | Harmonised CPI by ECOICOP | Eurostat |
| `hicp_constant_tax` | monthly | national | HICP under constant indirect-tax rates | Eurostat |
| `inflation` | monthly/annual | national/NUTS II | Log or percentage CPI/HICP change | derived |
| `productivity_growth` | annual/quarterly | national/region where available | Real GDP per hour or worker growth | AMECO / Eurostat / INE |
| `minimum_wage_bite` | semiannual/annual | region/industry | Share of full-time employees at RMMG | GEP/MTSSS |
| `labour_cost_share` | annual | industry | Labour compensation divided by relevant output/cost denominator | INE / Eurostat national accounts |
| `production_weight` | benchmark-year | category × industry | Industry contribution to a consumption category | INE supply-use/input-output tables |
| `cost_exposure` | policy event | region × category | Predicted minimum-wage-induced cost exposure | derived |
| `unemployment_rate` | monthly/quarterly | region | Labour-market slack | INE / Eurostat |
| `tourism_intensity` | monthly | region | Tourism exposure/control | INE |
| `imported_inflation` | monthly/quarterly | national | Import-price or deflator control | INE / Eurostat |
